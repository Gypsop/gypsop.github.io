Introduction

About Me

I am an interdisciplinary researcher working at the intersection of Urban Informatics, Mobile Computing & Smart Sensing, Spatial Data Science, and Applied AI. My core vision is to harness multi-source physical sensors — acoustic arrays, LiDAR, video, and smartphone imagery — alongside advanced spatial statistics and deep learning to achieve high-precision spatio-temporal understanding of high-density cities.

My research addresses human-centred challenges: calibration-free indoor positioning, real-time 3-D mapping, urban accessibility auditing, traffic emission estimation, and spatial equity for disadvantaged groups. I collaborate closely with the MIT Senseable City Lab, the Hong Kong Polytechnic University, and Politecnico di Milano.
